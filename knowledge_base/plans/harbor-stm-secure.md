## Pre-existing + look-back

- Pre-existing conditions are excluded unless modified by the optional Pre-Existing Conditions Rider [ES1, 2026-11-04; P1, 2026-11-04; D1 p6, p10]
- Look-back: 5 years before the effective date (advice, diagnosis, care, treatment or prescription); a pregnancy existing the day before the effective date is pre-existing [ES1, 2026-11-04]
- Without the rider, limitations apply separately to each new coverage period [P1, 2026-11-04; D1 p6]
- Pre-Existing Conditions Rider: extra premium; modifies the limit after the first coverage period on qualifying consecutive coverage; not immediate or complete; not available in Ohio [P1, 2026-11-04; D1 p6]

## Pre-existing + look-back

- Pre-existing conditions are excluded [P1, 2026-11-04; AHS p3, p12]
- Look-back: 1 year before the effective date (advice, care, treatment, prescription, or symptoms); a pregnancy existing the day before the effective date is pre-existing (not in TN) [AHS p12, read 2026-11-04]
- South Dakota: 12-month look-back; pregnancy existing on the effective date [AHS p12]
- With the renewable or consecutive purchase options, pre-existing conditions are covered after the first 12 months of coverage [AHS p7]

## Waiting periods

- Sickness: benefits begin after 7 days; the 7-day wait is waived if the application date is more than 7 days before the effective date [AHS p3 footnote 3, read 2026-11-04]
- Injury: coverage as soon as the next day [AHS p3]
- "No waiting period applies" is footnote 7 on the Pharmacy row [AHS p4]

## Exclusions & limitations

- Pre-existing conditions; mental illness or substance abuse (except the ASD benefit); pregnancy except complications [AHS p12-13]
- Surgery for ear tubes, tonsils, adenoids, hernia, sinuses or deviated septum; gastric bypass; weight control; joint replacement unless injury [AHS p13]
- Preventive or prophylactic care except as listed; dental and TMJ (not FL); vision; hearing devices; foot conditions; chronic pain; end stage renal disease [AHS p12-13]
- Outpatient prescription drugs except as covered; OTC drugs [AHS p14]
- Hazardous activities, felony, intoxication, intercollegiate or professional contact sports [AHS p13]

## Rx
